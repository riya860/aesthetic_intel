Aesthetic Intel v1.5.1 — Secure Business Deletion QA

Completed checks
- All application PHP files pass php -l syntax validation.
- All JavaScript files pass node --check.
- New delete route is Super Admin-only through require_admin().
- Delete confirmation uses CSRF protection.
- Current Super Admin password is verified against the authenticated user ID, active status, and super_admin role.
- Business-user passwords cannot authorize deletion.
- Explicit permanent-deletion acknowledgement is required.
- RESTRICT-linked business rows (AI extractions, Provider KPI imports, GBP entries, upload batches) are deleted before the final business cascade.
- The final business delete remains transactional; database errors roll back.
- Business uploads, Boulevard sync files, and the business logo are staged before DB deletion and restored if the DB transaction fails.
- Other business folders are never selected by the deletion path builder.
- Existing Super Admin business context is cleared when the currently viewed business is deleted.
- Incorrect password attempts and successful deletion are written to the audit log.
- No database schema change is required.
