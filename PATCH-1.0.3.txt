AESTHETIC INTEL v1.0.3

Adds the Google Business Profile manual-entry module.

Features:
- Manual GBP entry for interactions, calls, directions, website clicks, reviews, rating, and unanswered reviews.
- Saves each entry by business and reporting period.
- Calculates new weekly activity from cumulative GBP totals.
- Compares current weekly activity with the previous saved weekly activity when enough history exists.
- Review and unanswered-review interpretation.
- GBP history and detail pages.
- Business dashboard shortcut and latest GBP summary.

No reinstall is required. Database tables are created automatically.
