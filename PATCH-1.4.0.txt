Aesthetic Intel v1.4.0 — Provider KPI Dashboard Phase 2

Adds the management and coaching layer requested for Remedy provider performance:

- Actionable Opportunity Dashboard per provider
  - Total Revenue Opportunity Remaining
  - Revenue Needed to Reach Goal
  - Revenue Lost from Open Hours
  - Additional Patients Needed
  - Additional Productive Hours Needed
  - Average Ticket Increase Needed
  - Revenue Per Hour Needed to Hit Goal
  - Required Rate Across Open Hours
  - Remaining Appointment Capacity
- Clinic-wide opportunity summary and priority provider list
- Interactive provider trends for 90 days, 6 months, and 12 months
- Rankings for Production, Revenue/Hour, Utilization, Average Ticket, Membership Sales, Retail Sales, and New Patient Conversion
- KPI drill-down pages showing supporting drivers, source records, and 12-month history
- Coaching workspace with monthly reviews, wins, risks, opportunities, next-review dates, and action plans
- Action ownership, due dates, priority, status, completion, and deletion
- Provider read-only access to their own coaching review
- Leadership/Super Admin editing access; Data Uploaders cannot access confidential coaching notes
- New optional KPI inputs for New Patient Leads, New Patient Conversions, and New Patient Conversion Rate
- User-friendly responsive layouts and print-ready coaching/opportunity pages

Safety and compatibility:
- Existing Provider KPI providers, goals, imports, July/August test values, users, reports, Boulevard settings, and credentials are preserved.
- Database migrations run automatically after the first refresh.
- No installer is required.
- Phase 1 CSV files remain valid; the new template only adds optional columns.
