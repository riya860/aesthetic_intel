Aesthetic Intel v1.0.8
- Forces MM-DD-YYYY display and entry format across reporting periods and major date displays.
- Weekly remains a rolling 7-day period ending on the selected end date.
- Monthly remains month-to-date, from the first day of the selected month through the selected end date.
- Quarterly is now a rolling 3-month period ending on the selected end date.
- Yearly is now a rolling 12-month period ending on the selected end date.
- Server and browser use the same period rules.
